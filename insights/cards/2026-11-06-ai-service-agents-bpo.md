# Action card: ai-service-agents-bpo, 6 Nov 2026

**What changed.** TP (Teleperformance) reported Q3 2026 revenue of EUR 2,532m on 5 Nov: +1.0% reported, -1.2% like-for-like, the same as Q2. It cut its 2026 like-for-like revenue growth outlook to -1.5% to -2.5%, from 0% to +2%, citing an "accelerated decline in Trust and Safety"; management links part of the Americas Trust and Safety decline to faster automation. Margin (recurring EBITA about 14.6%) and cash-flow guidance were confirmed (https://www.tp.com/media/kcdch3fb/tp-press-release-q3-2026-revenue-eng.pdf).

**Rule.** A catalyst resolved against the pre-registered expectation. EV-20261105-TEP-2 expected the target to be kept (45% that it would be cut); it was cut. The surprise runs in the thesis's favour. Kill criterion 1 (TP H2 like-for-like +2% or more) is now out of reach. Related: BPO-1 hit (below +1.0%, 70%), EV-20261105-TEP-1 miss (at least 0%, 40%).

**Plan written in advance.** The thesis is a draft with no position and no tranches. A cut confirms the direction; the next test is BPO-2 (Concentrix guides fiscal 2027 at or below 0% constant currency, January) and BPO-3 (Indeed US customer-service index below 85 on 31 Jan).

**Strongest counter-argument.** The cut is pinned on one segment (Trust and Safety, where content-moderation clients are insourcing and automating), not on core customer care. Margins and cash were held, and TP says AI projects more than doubled to 1,400, so it may capture part of the automation spend itself.

**What would reverse it.** Core customer-experience like-for-like turning positive in Q4, Concentrix guiding to positive constant-currency growth, or the customer-service postings index rising for two quarters.

**Decision for John.** None needed now. Option: move the thesis from draft to active and set owner conviction.
