# Action card: power-grid-constraint, 29 Oct 2026

**What changed.** GE Vernova gas equipment backlog plus slot reservations reached 122 GW at Q3 2026 (from 116 GW), against a pre-registered 70% for at least 124 GW (GRID-1). Backlog rose 53 -> 64 GW; reservations fell 63 -> 58 GW as 13 GW converted to orders; 11 GW of new contracts; 5 GW shipped. Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001996810/000199681026000162/gevpressrelease3q26.htm

**Rule.** A catalyst resolved against the pre-registered expectation.

**Plan written in advance.** None specific to this forecast. Kill criteria checked: gas book-to-bill is well above 1.5 (16 GW ordered or converted against 5 GW shipped; Power orders $21.8bn against Power revenue of about $6.1bn), so not hit. No position change.

**Strongest counter-argument against reading this as weakness.** Orders were $29.3bn (+96% organic), 2026 guidance was raised, Electrification backlog is $42.2bn (+60%), and the company expects at least 130 GW by year-end. The forecast's own error was ignoring shipments (5 GW), which reduce the total.

**What would reverse the concern.** 130 GW or more at year-end, and Eaton (3 Nov) reporting Electrical Americas backlog up quarter on quarter (EV-20261103-ETN-2).
