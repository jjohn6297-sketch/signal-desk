# Action card: long-duration-bonds, 11 Nov 2026

**What changed.** US October core CPI rose 0.3% m/m (seasonally adjusted), the same as September; headline +0.4%, shelter +0.3%, energy +0.7% (BLS, https://www.bls.gov/news.release/cpi.htm?lv=true). The pre-registered EV-20261110-US-3 put 68.4% (raw 55%) on 0.2% or less; outcome no, Brier 0.47. CME FedWatch now has 88.8% on a 25bp December hike and 11.2% on 50bp, with no hold priced (https://ca.investing.com/news/economy-news/fed-rate-hike-odds-in-december-at-88-after-hot-cpi-cme-fedwatch-4948680). System conviction 35 -> 30.

**Rule.** A catalyst resolved against the pre-registered expectation.

**Plan written in advance.** None specific to this forecast. Kill criteria checked: 10-year 4.86% on 6 Nov (FRED DGS10; the GitHub job has no later value yet), well below 5.40% for three sessions; 5y5y 2.19% on 9 Nov, below 2.6%; core PCE and December FOMC guidance not yet known. No position change.

**Strongest counter-argument.** The bet is on long yields peaking as growth slows, not on one month of core CPI. Breakevens are steady (5y5y about 2.2%), so the market sees the hikes as containing inflation, which is what precedes a peak in long yields.

**What would reverse it.** A December FOMC that signals one more hike at most, a softer November CPI (10 Dec), and the 10-year staying below 5.31% (BOND-1).

**Note on the forecast.** The centre of published forecasts (about 0.23%) was close to the rounding line; 55% raw was reasonable, but recalibration lifted it to 68%. Worth checking at Sunday's review whether recalibration over-stretches near-50% calls.
